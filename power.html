What these columns mean
1D/route
Receiving first downs ÷ routes run. The best single read on a receiver: it counts the catches that keep drives alive and ignores the ones that don't. 12%+ is the league-winner line for a WR.
Tgt/route
How often he's thrown to when he actually runs a route. Around 20%+ is a featured receiver. Target share can't tell you this — it counts the snaps he sat out.
Routes
Pass routes run, estimated. See the note below.
Last 1 / 3 / 5
His last N games with routes — byes and inactives are skipped, not counted as zeros. Rates add up the window's first downs and routes and then divide, so one quiet game can't swing them the way an average of weekly rates would.
Greyed rows
Too few routes in the window for the rate to mean anything. No line, no percentile. Untick "enough routes to judge" to see them.
%ile
Where he ranks among players at his own position who clear that bar.
