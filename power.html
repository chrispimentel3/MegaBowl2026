How to read it

1st downs/route
Receiving first downs ÷ routes run. The single best read on a receiver: it rewards the catches that keep drives alive and ignores the ones that don't. 12%+ is the league-winner line for a WR.
Targets/route
How often he is thrown to when he actually runs a route. Around 20%+ is a featured receiver. This is what separates a real role from empty snaps — target share can't, because it counts the snaps he sat out.
Routes (est.)
Pass routes run, estimated. See below.
Window
The last N games he ran routes in — byes and inactives are skipped, not counted as zeros. Rates sum the top and bottom across the window and then divide, so one quiet game can't swing them the way an average of weekly rates would.
Greyed rows
Under the route minimum for the window, so the rates are noise. They get no flag and sit out the percentile. "Qualified only" hides them.
%ile
Where his rolling number ranks among qualified players at his own position.
