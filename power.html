What these columns mean
1D/route
Receiving first downs ÷ routes run. The best single read on a receiver: it counts the catches that keep drives alive and ignores the ones that don't. 12%+ is the league-winner line for a WR. Tight ends read lower — judge a TE against other TEs.
Tgt/route
How often he's thrown to when he actually runs a route. Around 20%+ is a featured receiver. Target share can't tell you this — it counts the snaps he sat out.
Routes/g
Pass routes per game, estimated. See the note below.
Last 1 / 3 / 5
His last N games with routes — byes and inactives are skipped, not counted as zeros. Rates add up the window's first downs and routes and then divide, so one quiet game can't swing them the way an average of weekly rates would.
Who's plotted
Only players with enough routes in the window for the rates to mean anything. Everyone else is in the sheet, greyed, with the routes he'd need.
